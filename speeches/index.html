Dallin H. Oaks

1932–
President of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints (since October 2025)

Lawyer, law professor, and former BYU president; former Utah Supreme Court justice; became the Church's eighteenth president in October 2025.

Callings
  • President of Brigham Young University (1971–1980)
  • Justice, Utah Supreme Court (1980–1984)
  • Quorum of the Twelve Apostles (1984–2018)
  • First Counselor in the First Presidency (2018–2025)
  • President of the Church (2025– )

Russell M. Nelson

1924–2025 · died September 27, 2025
Seventeenth President of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints (2018–2025)

Pioneering heart surgeon who helped make open-heart surgery possible; apostle for over thirty years before leading the Church. Died September 27, 2025, at 101.

Callings
  • Sunday School General President (1971–1979)
  • Quorum of the Twelve Apostles (1984–2018)
  • President of the Quorum of the Twelve (2015–2018)
  • President of the Church (2018–2025)

M. Russell Ballard

1928–2023 · died November 12, 2023
Acting President of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles (2018–2023)

Businessman in automotive, real estate, and investments before full-time Church service; called to the Seventy in 1976 and the Twelve in 1985. Died November 12, 2023.

Callings
  • Canada Toronto Mission President (1974–1976)
  • Seventy (1976–1985)
  • Quorum of the Twelve Apostles (1985–2023)
  • Acting President of the Quorum of the Twelve (2018–2023)
